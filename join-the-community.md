# Join the Community

This course is free and always will be. Every lesson, every script and every hands on exercise is here for anyone who wants to learn ServiceNow development.

If this course helps you, here are two ways to say thank you. Your support keeps new lessons coming and helps more people learn ServiceNow development for free.

## Join the community

Become a member of the TechTalk with Bill channel on YouTube and choose the level that suits you best. Each level shows its perks and its monthly price in your local currency.

[Join the community](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCMf7pje1x5iZkkEF-Y3PLSA/join)

Want Bill to personally review your final exercise? Choose the **Exam Navigator** level when you join. See [Get Help & Get Verified](support-and-verification.md) and [Submit Your Work for Verification](module-7-capstone/02-submit-for-verification.md) for details.

## Send a one time thank you

Prefer not to pay monthly? Open any video on the [TechTalk with Bill channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCMf7pje1x5iZkkEF-Y3PLSA), click the three dots (**...**) under the video and choose **Thanks**. Your message appears as a highlighted comment.

Thank you for being part of this community.

Bill
