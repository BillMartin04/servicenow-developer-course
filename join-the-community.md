# Join the Community

This course is free and always will be. Every lesson, every script and every hands on exercise is here for anyone who wants to learn ServiceNow development.

If this course is helping you, please join the TechTalk with Bill community on YouTube. Your membership keeps new lessons coming and helps more people learn ServiceNow development for free.

<a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCMf7pje1x5iZkkEF-Y3PLSA/join" class="button primary">Join the Community</a>

## Why join

- **Keep the course free.** Members fund the time it takes to record, write and update every lesson.
- **Learn alongside other developers.** Share what you build, ask questions and see how others solve the same problems.
- **Choose the level that suits you.** Each level shows its perks and its monthly price in your local currency.

## Get your work reviewed with Exam Navigator

Doing the exercises is one thing. Knowing they are right is another. Choose the **Exam Navigator** level when you join and Bill personally reviews your work.

As an Exam Navigator member you can:

- Submit your [final exercise](module-7-capstone/00-final-exercise.md) for review against the [scoring rubric](module-7-capstone/01-scoring-rubric.md).
- Have your module hands on work checked when you are not sure it is correct.
- Ask questions in member only posts and get priority answers.
- Get feedback on architecture decisions, the part that separates a developer from an architect.

<a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCMf7pje1x5iZkkEF-Y3PLSA/join" class="button primary">Become an Exam Navigator member</a>

Full submission steps are on [Submit Your Work for Verification](module-7-capstone/02-submit-for-verification.md).
